I am a theoretical condensed matter physicist working on two-dimensional materials, in particular moiré superlattices and twisted multilayer graphene. I study how structure beyond the atomic lattice, such as moiré patterns and strain, reshapes electrons and lattice vibrations, and how this shows up in electrical and thermal transport. My tools are analytical continuum models and large-scale numerical calculations.

I received my PhD in physics from Osaka University in 2024, advised by Mikito Koshino, and stayed there as a postdoctoral researcher until 2025. Since November 2025 I have been at BRIN in Indonesia. See my research for more.
